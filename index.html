03

Field Notes

I came to data analytics by way of anthropology — a discipline built on close observation, careful documentation, and drawing sound conclusions from incomplete evidence. That training turned out to be surprisingly good preparation for data work: both start with a question, both demand rigor before a conclusion, and both are really about telling an honest story from what the evidence shows.

Today I work across the analytics stack — SQL, Python, Power BI, and Tableau — building dashboards and reports that turn scattered data into decisions people can actually act on. I'm currently extending that foundation into data science and AI-assisted automation.

Based in Lucknow, India — open to Data Analyst and Data Science roles.
